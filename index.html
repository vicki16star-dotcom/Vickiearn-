FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a joining fee?

No. Creating an account does not require a joining fee.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on eligible activities, successful completion, applicable rules and available program revenue.

What is the current minimum withdrawal?

The current displayed minimum withdrawal threshold is ₦5,000. Check your dashboard for the current threshold before requesting a withdrawal.

Where can I see my activity?

After signing in, your dashboard is where you view your wallet, tasks, referrals and withdrawal status.
